# Veloce Motors – Design System

## 1. Brand Direction
Veloce Motors should feel premium, confident, and trustworthy. The visual language should communicate performance, luxury, and precision without feeling flashy or exaggerated.

Core qualities:
- premium
- modern
- high-end
- trustworthy
- performance-focused

## 2. Visual Style
- Dark luxury palette with rich contrast
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Spacious layout with generous padding
- Premium automotive imagery
- Clean cards, sharp edges, and minimal clutter
- High contrast call-to-action buttons

## 3. Color Palette
Primary colors:
- Midnight Black: #0B0E12
- Graphite: #171C22
- Gold Accent: #D4AF68
- Silver: #C7CCD1
- White: #F7F7F5
- Muted Gray: #A5A9AF

Usage:
- background: midnight black / deep graphite
- headings: white
- supporting text: light gray
- highlights: gold accent
- buttons: gold or white depending on contrast

## 4. Typography
Recommended fonts:
- Headings: Inter
- Arabic text: Tajawal
- Sans fallback: system UI

Type scale:
- Hero title: large, bold, uppercase or title case
- Section titles: strong, high contrast
- Body copy: clean, readable sans-serif
- Buttons: medium weight, compact uppercase styling

## 5. Layout System
- Max content width: 1200px
- Large margins between sections
- Consistent 8px spacing scale
- Use cards with soft border, dark background, and subtle glow/shadow
- Keep content aligned and visually calm

Suggested spacing scale:
- 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 96px

## 6. Section Design

### Header
- slim dark bar
- logo on left
- nav centered or aligned right
- EN / ع language toggle
- primary CTA button: “Test Drive”

### Hero
- dark cinematic background image
- large headline
- short luxury marketing text
- two CTA buttons
- trust stat cards below

### Inventory
- 3–6 product cards in a grid
- image at top
- title, spec list, price
- premium button “View Details”
- consistent card height

### Why Us
- 4 cards with numbered headings
- small premium icon style
- benefit-driven content
- trust-building language

### Reviews
- 3 testimonial cards
- short quotes
- client name and location
- simple avatar circle

### Contact / Test Drive
- two-column layout
- left: contact info
- right: form
- confirmation/error message visible under submit button

## 7. Components

### Buttons
Primary:
- gold background
- high contrast
- rounded or slightly rounded corners
- hover state: brighter gold or subtle lift

Secondary:
- transparent dark/outlined
- white or gold border
- hover glow

### Inputs
- dark filled fields
- light border on focus
- clear labels
- visible error states

### Cards
- dark background
- subtle border
- soft shadow
- consistent padding
- premium hover reveal

## 8. Navigation
- minimal, clean nav items
- strong hover underline or text brightness
- mobile menu becomes a slide-down or drawer
- nav remains readable in both English and Arabic
- skip link remains visible for keyboard users

## 9. Responsive Behavior
Mobile-first:
- stacked hero content
- single-column cards on small screens
- CTA buttons full width when necessary
- nav collapses into menu button
- text remains large and readable

Desktop:
- multi-column grids
- more spacious hero layout
- horizontal navigation
- wider card rows

## 10. RTL Design
Arabic mode must support:
- right-to-left layout
- mirrored layout alignment
- text direction adjustments
- Arabic typography with readable line height
- form and nav alignment that feel natural in RTL

## 11. Accessibility
- high contrast text on dark backgrounds
- visible focus rings on interactive elements
- buttons and links must have clear labels
- form controls must have labels
- status messages should be readable and announced
- avoid low-contrast text or overly decorative fonts

## 12. Design Principles
- clarity before decoration
- trust through polish
- luxury through restraint
- consistency across all sections
- readability above style effects
- premium experience on all devices

## 13. Final Design Outcome
The page should feel like a premium automotive brand showroom: elegant, trustworthy, and conversion-focused.
